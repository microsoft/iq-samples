## Description

ZavaCore Smart Cleats are professional-grade athletic footwear with embedded nano sensors that capture live biomechanical performance data during training and competition. Designed for professional sports teams, collegiate programs, and elite training facilities, the Smart Cleats extend Zava's smart mesh ecosystem from the upper body to the ground contact interface — completing the full-body biometric picture.

Each cleat integrates ZavaCore Smart Mesh into the upper construction and sole plate, with dedicated sensor arrays positioned at the heel, midfoot, forefoot, and toe box. These sensors capture ground reaction force, foot strike patterns, pressure distribution, plantar flexion angles, and cleat-to-surface interaction metrics in real time. The data streams through the same priority channel used by Elite and Systems apparel, enabling unified full-body telemetry when paired with ZavaCore garments.

The Smart Cleats are purpose-built for real-time coaching feedback and injury prevention analytics. Coaching staff receive live dashboards showing stride asymmetry, load distribution imbalances, and fatigue-induced gait changes — allowing intervention before injury occurs. Post-session reports provide detailed biomechanical breakdowns for performance optimization and rehabilitation tracking.

Cleat plate configurations are available for natural grass (molded studs), artificial turf (short rubber studs), and firm ground (bladed studs). Upper construction uses reinforced ZavaCore Smart Mesh with a thermoplastic heel counter for stability and lockdown.

## Care & Maintenance

- Wipe clean with damp cloth after use. Do not submerge in water or machine wash.
- Allow to air dry at room temperature. Do not use direct heat sources.
- Sensor recalibration is recommended every 200 hours of use or seasonally, whichever comes first. Recalibration is performed by Zava's on-site or ship-in service team.
- Stud replacement kits available for molded and bladed configurations.
