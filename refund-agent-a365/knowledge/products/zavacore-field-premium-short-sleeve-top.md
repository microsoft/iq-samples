## Description

The ZavaCore Field Premium Short Sleeve Top is an entry point into Zava's smart materials ecosystem. Built with ZavaCore Smart Mesh — a graphene-infused performance knit with embedded nano sensors — this top delivers real-time biometric awareness in a lightweight, breathable athletic silhouette. Designed for training, competition, and everyday active wear, the Premium Short Sleeve combines advanced material science with athlete-tested comfort.

The garment features 4-way stretch construction for unrestricted movement, flatlock seams to eliminate chafing during high-intensity activity, and Zava's proprietary moisture-wicking finish that pulls sweat away from the skin for rapid evaporation. The graphene-infused fibers provide inherent anti-odor properties and quick-dry performance, keeping athletes fresh across extended sessions.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones.
- Avoid prolonged contact with abrasive surfaces to preserve graphene mesh integrity.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
