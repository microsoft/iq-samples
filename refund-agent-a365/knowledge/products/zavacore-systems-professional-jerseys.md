## Description

ZavaCore Systems Professional Jerseys are custom-manufactured smart apparel produced exclusively for professional sports teams, athletic leagues, and elite training programs. Each order is engineered to the client's specifications — custom team colors, logos, sponsor placements, player numbering, and sensor configurations — while leveraging the full capability of Zava's ZavaCore Smart Mesh platform.

Unlike the consumer Field line, Systems jerseys are built to triple-layer ZavaCore Smart Mesh construction, providing maximum sensor density, structural durability for high-contact sports, and the most comprehensive biometric telemetry available in any wearable garment. The triple-layer design enables sensor redundancy — if one layer's sensors are compromised by impact or abrasion, the remaining layers maintain full data capture without interruption.

Sensor configurations are customized per client. Standard packages include heart rate, respiration, skin temperature, and muscle activation. Extended packages add impact detection, positional tracking integration, and real-time injury risk scoring. All telemetry is streamed through dedicated team data channels compatible with major sports analytics platforms.

Zava's B2B team manages the full lifecycle: design consultation, prototype fitting, production, quality assurance, and ongoing sensor recalibration support. Typical lead time from design approval to delivery is 8–12 weeks.

## Sizing & Customization

Systems jerseys are produced in custom size runs based on team roster measurements. Standard size ranges (S–3XL) are available, with bespoke sizing for individual athletes upon request. All customization — including color matching (Pantone), logo placement, and sensor zone configuration — is finalized during the design consultation phase.
