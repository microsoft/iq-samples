## Description

ZavaCore Smart Mesh is Zava's proprietary graphene-infused fabric with embedded nano sensors — the foundational technology behind every product in the ZavaCore ecosystem. Developed at Zava's Woodinville, WA headquarters, Smart Mesh represents the convergence of advanced materials science, textile engineering, and embedded systems design into a single unified material platform.

At its core, ZavaCore Smart Mesh is a woven graphene-fiber fabric where each fiber strand contains a hexagonal lattice of carbon atoms arranged in a monolayer sheet structure. This hexagonal lattice provides the mechanical foundation — extraordinary tensile strength (130 PGa), exceptional thermal conductivity (3,000–5,000 W/mK), and controlled electrical conductivity (10⁻⁶ S/m) — while remaining flexible enough to drape and stretch like conventional textile fibers.

## Nano Sensor Integration

The defining innovation of ZavaCore Smart Mesh is how nano sensors are coupled directly to the graphene fiber network. Rather than being attached to the fabric surface (as with conventional wearable sensors), Zava's nano sensors are woven into the mesh at the fiber level during the manufacturing process. Each sensor node bonds to the hexagonal lattice through covalent coupling points, creating a permanent mechanical and electrical connection that survives stretching, washing, and repeated mechanical stress.

The sensor nodes are distributed across the fabric in a configurable grid pattern. Sensor density varies by product tier:

| Product Tier | Mesh Layers | Relative Sensor Density |
|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Field Premium (B2C) | Single | 1× (baseline) |
| Field Elite (B2C) | Double | 2× |
| Systems (B2B) | Triple | 3× (with redundancy) |

## Conductive Pathways & Signal Architecture

Communication between sensor nodes and the garment's data output interface relies on conductive pathways embedded within the graphene fiber weave. These pathways are formed by selectively doping specific fiber strands with metallic nanoparticles during the spinning process, creating dedicated signal and power conduits that run alongside the structural fibers without compromising the fabric's mechanical properties.

The architecture uses a distributed power and signal bus:

- **Power Bus**: A low-voltage distributed power network that harvests energy from body heat (thermoelectric effect) and supplemental motion (piezoelectric micro-generators at key flex points). No external battery is required for standard biometric capture.
- **Signal Bus**: A multiplexed data bus that aggregates sensor readings across the garment and routes them to the data output interface — typically located at the garment's lower hem or waistband. The signal bus supports both continuous streaming and event-triggered burst modes.
- **Data Output Interface**: A near-field communication (NFC) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) hybrid module embedded in a reinforced fabric pocket. This module bridges the on-garment signal bus to paired external devices (smartphones, coaching tablets, team analytics platforms).

## Manufacturing

All ZavaCore Smart Mesh is produced at Zava's vertically integrated facility in Woodinville, WA. The manufacturing process spans graphene fiber synthesis, nano sensor fabrication, weaving, finishing (antimicrobial coating, hydrophobic treatment), and quality assurance. Zava controls the full supply chain from raw graphene feedstock to finished fabric rolls, ensuring consistency and traceability across production lots.
