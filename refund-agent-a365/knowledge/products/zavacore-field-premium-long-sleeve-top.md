## Description

The ZavaCore Field Premium Long Sleeve Top extends Zava's premium smart apparel line into full-arm coverage, ideal for cooler conditions, outdoor training, and athletes who prefer additional sun protection during extended sessions. Built on the same ZavaCore Smart Mesh platform as the short sleeve variant, this top adds compression-mapped muscle support zones and day-to-night reflective trim for visibility in low-light environments.

The compression mapping follows anatomical muscle groups along the arms, shoulders, and torso, providing graduated support that reduces fatigue and improves proprioceptive feedback during repetitive movements. The reflective trim is integrated into the seam lines and does not affect the garment's hand feel or flexibility.

Like all Premium tier products, the long sleeve top features 4-way stretch graphene-infused performance knit, flatlock seam construction, moisture-wicking and quick-dry performance, and inherent anti-odor properties from the graphene fiber treatment.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones or reflective trim.
- Avoid prolonged contact with abrasive surfaces to preserve graphene mesh integrity.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
