## Description

The ZavaCore Field Elite Short Sleeve Top represents the pinnacle of Zava's direct-to-consumer smart apparel. Engineered for serious athletes and performance-minded individuals, the Elite tier upgrades the proven Premium platform with enhanced sensor density and priority data stream capabilities, delivering richer biometric telemetry with lower latency.

Where the Premium tier deploys a single-layer mesh with standard sensor spacing, the Elite Short Sleeve uses a double-layer ZavaCore Smart Mesh construction that doubles the number of embedded nano sensors per square centimeter. This increased density enables finer-grained biometric capture — heart rate variability, skin temperature gradients, and micro-movement analysis — all streamed via the priority data channel to paired devices with minimal delay.

The garment retains all core ZavaCore features: 4-way stretch graphene-infused knit, flatlock seam construction, moisture-wicking and quick-dry finish, anti-odor graphene fiber treatment, and UPF 452 sun protection. The Elite tier adds compression-mapped muscle support as standard and uses bonded hems for a cleaner, more aerodynamic profile.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones.
- Store flat or on wide hangers to preserve double-layer mesh alignment.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
