## Description

The ZavaCore Field Elite Long Sleeve Top is the most advanced consumer garment in Zava's product line. Combining full-arm coverage with Elite-tier enhanced sensor density, this top delivers real-time biometric tracking with a performance overlay that maps exertion, recovery, and muscle engagement across the entire upper body.

The real-time performance overlay is the defining feature of the Elite Long Sleeve. By leveraging the double-layer ZavaCore Smart Mesh and its increased nano sensor count, the garment captures continuous biometric streams — heart rate, skin temperature, muscle activation patterns, and respiration rate — and feeds them through the priority data channel to paired coaching apps and wearable displays. Athletes and coaches receive live visual overlays showing effort zones, fatigue indicators, and recovery windows during training and competition.

Additional features include compression-mapped muscle support along the arms, shoulders, and torso, day-to-night reflective trim integrated into the seam lines, and bonded hems for a streamlined fit. The long sleeve construction provides enhanced thermal regulation in variable conditions while maintaining the breathability expected from ZavaCore Smart Mesh.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones or reflective trim.
- Store flat or on wide hangers to preserve double-layer mesh alignment.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
