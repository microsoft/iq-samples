## Description

The ZavaCore Smart Socks are Zava's next-generation wearable, extending the ZavaCore Smart Mesh platform into purpose-built athletic hosiery for foot pressure mapping, gait analysis, and injury prevention. By bringing nano sensor coverage to the foot — the body's primary interface with the ground — the Smart Socks complete the head-to-toe biometric ecosystem when paired with existing ZavaCore apparel and Smart Cleats.

The socks will use a custom-knit ZavaCore Smart Mesh construction optimized for the foot's complex geometry, with high-density sensor zones at the heel, arch, ball of foot, and individual toe pads. The sensor array will capture real-time plantar pressure distribution, foot strike timing, pronation/supination angles, and cumulative load metrics — data that is foundational to gait analysis and early injury detection for conditions such as plantar fasciitis, stress fractures, and Achilles tendinopathy.

## Target Capabilities

- **Foot Pressure Mapping**: Continuous real-time pressure distribution across the plantar surface, visualized as heat maps in paired coaching and fitness apps.
- **Gait Analysis**: Stride symmetry, foot strike classification (heel/midfoot/forefoot), contact time, and cadence tracking across walking, running, and sport-specific movements.
- **Injury Prevention**: Algorithmic risk scoring based on cumulative load, asymmetry detection, and deviation from baseline movement patterns. Configurable alerts for coaches and athletic trainers.
- **Compatibility**: Full data integration with ZavaCore Elite apparel and Smart Cleats for unified full-body biomechanical telemetry.

## Go-to-Market Plan

### Phase 1 — B2C Field Premium (Q2 2026)

Initial launch as a consumer product in the Field Premium tier. Available in all 6 standard ZavaCore colorways, sizes S–XL for Men's, Women's, and Youth. Expected MSRP range: $39–$49 per pair. Distribution through zava.com, select specialty athletic retailers, and existing Zava retail partners.

### Phase 2 — B2B Systems (Q4 2026)

Following consumer launch validation, introduce a B2B Systems variant with double-layer mesh, enhanced sensor density, and team-configurable analytics integration. Target clients: professional sports teams, collegiate athletic programs, and sports medicine clinics already using ZavaCore Systems products. Contract pricing aligned with existing B2B structures.

## Risks & Open Items

- **Sensor durability in high-moisture environment**: Foot-specific moisture and friction loads exceed those of torso garments. Extended wear testing underway with target completion Q4 2025.
- **Toe sensor resolution**: Individual toe pad sensor accuracy at small scale requires further miniaturization of nano sensor nodes. R&D prototype iteration ongoing.
- **Regulatory considerations**: Medical-adjacent injury prevention claims may require regulatory review in certain markets. Legal review initiated.
- **Smart Cleat integration**: Ensuring sensor signal clarity when Smart Socks are worn inside Smart Cleats (potential signal interference from dual sensor arrays). Cross-product testing planned for Q1 2026.
