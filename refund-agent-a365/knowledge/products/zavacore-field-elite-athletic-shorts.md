## Description

The ZavaCore Field Elite Athletic Shorts bring Zava's smart mesh technology to lower-body performance wear. Designed for high-intensity training, field sports, and competitive athletics, these shorts combine the Elite tier's enhanced sensor density with a construction optimized for unrestricted leg movement and thermal comfort.

Built with double-layer ZavaCore Smart Mesh, the shorts feature graphene-infused performance knit with 4-way stretch and an advanced moisture-wicking system that manages heat buildup during sustained exertion. Contour piping along the side panels provides structural definition without restricting range of motion, while compression-mapped muscle support targets the quadriceps and hamstrings to reduce vibration fatigue and improve proprioceptive feedback.

The embedded nano sensors in the Elite shorts capture lower-body biometrics including stride cadence, muscle activation symmetry, and skin temperature — data that pairs with the Elite tops to provide full-body performance telemetry. An internal drawcord waistband with a silicone grip strip ensures a secure fit during dynamic movement.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones.
- Store flat to preserve double-layer mesh alignment and compression mapping.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
