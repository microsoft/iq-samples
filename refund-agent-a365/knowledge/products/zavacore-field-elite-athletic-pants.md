## Description

The ZavaCore Field Elite Athletic Pants deliver full-length lower-body coverage with the complete suite of Elite-tier smart mesh capabilities. Engineered for training sessions, warm-ups, recovery work, and cooler-weather competition, these pants provide continuous biometric telemetry from hip to ankle using Zava's enhanced-density nano sensor array.

Constructed from double-layer ZavaCore Smart Mesh, the pants feature graphene-infused performance knit with 4-way stretch and compression-mapped muscle support targeting the quadriceps, hamstrings, and calves. The full-length design captures a broader biometric profile than the Elite shorts, including knee flexion patterns, calf engagement metrics, and full lower-limb thermal mapping — making them the preferred choice for athletes focused on comprehensive movement analysis.

The pants include an internal drawcord waistband with silicone grip, zippered ankle cuffs for easy on/off over footwear, and a rear zip pocket for device storage. Reflective trim along the lower leg provides visibility in low-light training environments. The tapered silhouette reduces fabric drag while maintaining the ease of movement required for explosive athletic performance.

## Care Instructions

- Machine wash cold, gentle cycle. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
- Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on sensor zones or reflective trim.
- Close ankle zippers before washing to prevent snag damage.
- Store flat or folded to preserve double-layer mesh alignment.
- Sensor calibration resets automatically after each wash cycle.
