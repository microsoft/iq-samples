## 1. Purpose

## 3. Testing and Acceptance Criteria

- **Incoming Quality Level (IQL):** AQL 0.65, Level II per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4.
- **Functional Testing:** 100% of sensors must pass powered-on functional validation prior to shipment.
- **Environmental Stress Screening:** Each lot must include 50 units subjected to thermal cycling (-40°C to 85°C, 100 cycles) with zero failures.
- **Documentation:** Each shipment must include test reports, certificates of conformance, and lot-level traceability records.
- **Rejection Protocol:** Non-conforming lots are quarantined and returned via RMA within 10 business days. NanoTech Dynamics bears return freight costs.

Expedited orders (< 4 weeks) incur a 15% surcharge and are subject to capacity confirmation within 48 hours.

## 5. Packaging Requirements

- All sensors must be shipped in ESD-safe, moisture-barrier packaging per IPC/JEDEC J-STD-033D (Moisture Sensitivity Level 3).
- Desiccant packs and humidity indicator cards are required in each sealed bag.
- Reels must be vacuum-sealed with a maximum shelf life labeling of 24 months from seal date.
- Outer cartons must include lot number, quantity, manufacture date, and Zava PO reference on a scannable barcode label.

## 6. Supplier Performance Metrics

NanoTech Dynamics is evaluated quarterly against the following KPIs:

| Metric | Target | Minimum Acceptable |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| On-Time Delivery | ≥ 98% | 95% |
| Lot Acceptance Rate | ≥ 99.5% | 98% |
| RMA Turnaround | ≤ 10 business days | 15 business days |
| Engineering Change Notification Lead Time | ≥ 90 days | 60 days |
| Corrective Action Response | ≤ 5 business days | 10 business days |

Sustained performance below minimum acceptable thresholds for two consecutive quarters triggers a formal Supplier Corrective Action Request (SCAR) and may result in qualification of an alternate source.
