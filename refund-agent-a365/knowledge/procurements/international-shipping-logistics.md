## 1. Purpose

This guide establishes procurement requirements and service-level expectations for international shipping partners transporting Zava Inc.'s raw materials, components, and finished goods between global facilities (Woodinville WA, St. Louis MO, Charlotte NC, Vancouver BC, Montreal QC, Bergamo Italy, Borås Sweden) and supplier/customer locations worldwide.

## 2. Carrier Selection Criteria

All international carriers must meet the following qualifications before being added to Zava's carrier panel:

- **Licensing:** Valid IATA (air), NVOCC/FMC (ocean), or equivalent national licensing for all operating jurisdictions.
- **Insurance:** Minimum cargo insurance of $2,000,000 per shipment; errors and omissions coverage of $5,000,000.
- **Track Record:** Minimum 5 years of experience in international logistics with at least 2 current clients in advanced materials, electronics, or textile industries.
- **Technology:** Must support real-time shipment tracking with API integration to Zava's supply chain management platform. EDI 214 (shipment status) and EDI 990 (response to load tender) capabilities required.
- **Sustainability:** Carrier must report Scope 1 and Scope 3 emissions per shipment and participate in Zava's annual carbon footprint review.

## 3. Temperature-Controlled Shipping Requirements

ZavaCore Smart Mesh graphene composite materials are temperature-sensitive during transport. Degradation of the graphene-polymer interface can occur outside the specified range.

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Temperature Range | 10°C – 30°C (50°F – 86°F) |
| Humidity | ≤ 60% RH |
| Monitoring | Continuous IoT-enabled data loggers (readings every 15 minutes) |
| Excursion Protocol | Any deviation > 2°C outside range for > 30 minutes triggers automatic alert to Zava Supply Chain Operations |
| Documentation | Temperature log must accompany every shipment; digital copy uploaded to Zava's logistics portal within 24 hours of delivery |

Carriers are responsible for providing reefer containers (ocean), climate-controlled trailers (ground), and temperature-controlled ULD containers (air) at their own cost when transporting temperature-sensitive materials.

## 4. Customs and Duties Handling

- Zava's preferred customs broker (**Global Trade Compliance Partners, Seattle WA**) must be used for all US imports. Regional brokers may be pre-approved for EU and Canadian entries.
- Carriers must provide accurate commercial invoices, packing lists, and HS tariff classification codes at least 48 hours prior to arrival at the port of entry.
- All graphene materials are classified under HS Code 3801.90 (other artificial graphite preparations) or 2803.00 (carbon) depending on form factor. Carriers must verify classification against the destination country's tariff schedule.
- Duty drawback documentation must be retained for 5 years per US Customs and Border Protection requirements.

## 6. Insurance Requirements

- All shipments valued over $50,000 must carry "All Risk" marine/transit insurance per Institute Cargo Clauses (A).
- Zava must be named as loss payee on all cargo insurance certificates.
- Carriers must provide proof of insurance annually and upon any policy change or renewal.

## 7. Dispute Resolution

- Freight billing disputes must be raised within 30 days of invoice date via Zava's logistics portal.
- Cargo damage claims must be filed within 14 days of delivery with photographic evidence and the temperature/condition log.
- Unresolved disputes exceeding $25,000 shall be escalated to mediation under the rules of the American Arbitration Association. International disputes follow ICC Arbitration Rules, seated in Seattle, WA.
