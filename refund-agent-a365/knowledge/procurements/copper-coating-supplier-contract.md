## 1. Parties

This Contract Summary covers the active supply agreement between **Zava Inc.**, Woodinville, WA, USA, and **CuTech Industries Co., Ltd.**, 42 Teheran-ro 87-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul 06164, South Korea (Master Agreement CU-ZV-2025-017, executed September 1, 2025).

## 2. Product Description

CuTech supplies a proprietary **copper nano-coating suspension** used to impart antimicrobial properties to Zava's ZavaCore Smart Mesh fabrics. The coating is applied during the post-weave finishing stage and is integral to Zava's product claims regarding odor control and hygiene performance.

## 3. Material Purity and Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Active Copper Content | ≥ 98.0% (by weight of metallic component) |
| Nanoparticle Size | 40–80 nm median diameter (DLS verified) |
| Suspension Medium | Aqueous ethanol solution (70/30 water/ethanol by volume) |
| Concentration | 5.0% ± 0.2% w/v copper nanoparticles |
| Antimicrobial Efficacy | ≥ 99.9% reduction of S. aureus and E. coli per ISO 20743 |
| Shelf Life | 12 months from manufacture date (stored at 15–25°C, sealed) |
| Appearance | Dark reddish-brown, homogeneous suspension; no visible sedimentation |

## 4. Application Method Specifications

CuTech's copper nano-coating is validated for the following application methods on ZavaCore Smart Mesh:

- **Pad-Dry-Cure Process:** Fabric passes through a padding mangle at 80% wet pickup, dried at 100°C for 3 minutes, cured at 150°C for 2 minutes. This is the primary production method.
- **Spray Coating (secondary):** Ultrasonic spray at 0.5 mL/cm², followed by IR drying at 110°C for 90 seconds. Used for localized application zones.

CuTech provides application parameter guidelines and annual on-site calibration support at Zava's Woodinville WA and Bergamo Italy facilities at no additional cost.

## 5. Batch Testing Protocols

- **Pre-Shipment Testing (CuTech responsibility):** Each production batch must be tested for nanoparticle size distribution (DLS), copper content (ICP-OES), pH, viscosity, and antimicrobial efficacy (abbreviated ISO 20743 screen). Full test report and Certificate of Analysis accompany each shipment.
- **Incoming Inspection (Zava responsibility):** Zava performs copper content verification (XRF) and pH/viscosity spot checks on 10% of received containers. Any out-of-spec result triggers full lot testing.
- **Wash Durability Testing:** Joint quarterly validation — CuTech and Zava test coated fabric samples for antimicrobial retention after 50 wash cycles per ISO 6330. Target: ≥ 95% efficacy retention.

## 7. Exclusivity Terms

- **Geographic Exclusivity:** CuTech grants Zava exclusive supply rights for copper nano-coating suspensions in the **athletic and performance apparel** market segment within North America and the European Union.
- **Duration:** Exclusivity is maintained for the term of the agreement, contingent on Zava meeting a minimum annual purchase commitment of 10,000 liters.
- **Carve-Outs:** CuTech retains the right to supply copper nano-coatings to the medical device, industrial filtration, and HVAC markets without restriction.
- **Non-Compete:** Zava agrees not to develop or procure a substantially similar copper nano-coating formulation from an alternate source during the exclusivity period, except for R&D evaluation quantities not exceeding 200 liters annually.

## 8. Term and Termination

- **Term:** 3 years (September 1, 2025 – August 31, 2028), with automatic 1-year renewals.
- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with 180 days' written notice.
- **Termination for Cause:** Immediate termination upon material breach, insolvency, or failure to meet specifications for 3 consecutive shipments.
