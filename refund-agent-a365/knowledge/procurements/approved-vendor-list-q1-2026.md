## 3. Vendor Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| **Active** | Fully qualified, meets all performance and compliance requirements. |
| **Probationary** | Active but under corrective action; enhanced monitoring in effect. |
| **Suspended** | PO issuance prohibited pending resolution of non-conformance or audit finding. |
| **Inactive** | Previously approved; contract expired or voluntarily deactivated. Not authorized for new POs. |

## 4. Change Control

- Additions to the AVL require vendor qualification audit, reference checks, and approval by VP of Procurement.
- Removals are executed upon contract expiration, sustained poor performance, or compliance violations, per the Vendor Management Policy (POL-PROC-001).
- Emergency sole-source additions may be granted by VP of Procurement with a 90-day qualification deadline.
