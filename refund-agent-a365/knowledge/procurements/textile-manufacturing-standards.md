## 1. Purpose

This document defines the quality, inspection, and ethical compliance standards required of all textile manufacturing partners producing cut-and-sew garments for Zava Inc. These standards apply to all garment categories incorporating ZavaCore Smart Mesh, including performance tops, compression wear, and outerwear.

## 2. Cut-and-Sew Quality Standards

### 2.1 Defect Rate Tolerance

- **Maximum Acceptable Defect Rate:** 0.5% (5 defects per 1,000 units) across all defect categories.
- **Critical Defects (zero tolerance):** Sensor pocket misalignment > 1 mm, exposed conductive traces, broken or shorted sensor leads, and any defect affecting garment electrical continuity.
- **Major Defects:** Skipped stitches > 3 consecutive, seam puckering > 2 mm deviation, incorrect sizing beyond ±0.5 cm tolerance.
- **Minor Defects:** Loose threads (non-structural), slight color variation within Delta E ≤ 1.5.

### 2.2 Seam and Stitch Specifications

| Specification | Requirement |
|---------------|-------------|
| Primary Seam Type | 4-thread flatlock (ISO 4916 Type 607) |
| Stitch Density | 14–16 stitches per inch |
| Seam Strength | ≥ 90 N (per ISO 13935-2) |
| Seam Allowance | 8 mm ± 1 mm |
| Thread Type | Zava-approved bonded nylon 6.6 (Tex 45) |
| Needle Type | Ballpoint (SUK), size 70/10 for Smart Mesh panels |

Flatlock seams are mandatory on all skin-contact panels to prevent sensor displacement and ensure wearer comfort. Conventional overlock seams are acceptable only on non-sensor structural panels.

### 2.3 Thread and Adhesive Specifications

- **Thread:** Bonded nylon 6.6, Tex 45 weight, color-matched to garment body. Must pass 50-wash colorfastness test (ISO 105-C06, minimum Grade 4).
- **Adhesive (sensor pocket bonding):** Zava-specified heat-activated polyurethane film, activation temperature 130°C ± 5°C, bond strength ≥ 15 N/25 mm (per ASTM D1876).
- **No substitutions** of thread or adhesive materials without written approval from Zava Textile Operations.

## 3. Color Matching

- All fabrics and trims must be **Pantone-certified** with a color tolerance of Delta E ≤ 1.0 (CIE Lab, D65 illuminant).
- Lab dip approvals require submission of 3 swatches minimum, evaluated under D65, TL84, and CWF light sources to ensure no metameric failure.
- Production lots must include a retained sample swatch archived for 18 months.

## 4. Inspection Protocols

- **In-Line Inspection:** Continuous monitoring at each major workstation; defect logs updated hourly.
- **Pre-Shipment Inspection:** AQL 2.5 (Level II, ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) for general defects; AQL 0.65 for critical defects.
- **Electrical Continuity Testing:** 100% of finished garments containing sensor arrays must pass a powered-on circuit continuity check prior to packing.
- Zava reserves the right to conduct unannounced audits at any manufacturing facility with 48 hours' notice.

## 5. Ethical Labor Practices

All manufacturing partners must comply with the following:

- **No child labor.** Minimum working age of 16 years (or local legal minimum, whichever is higher).
- **No forced or compulsory labor** of any kind.
- **Working hours** shall not exceed 60 hours per week, with at least one rest day per seven-day period.
- **Living wages** must meet or exceed local legal minimums and industry benchmarks per the Fair Labor Association guidelines.
- Partners must hold or be actively pursuing **SA8000** or **WRAP** certification.
- Zava conducts annual third-party social compliance audits. Failure to remediate findings within 90 days results in contract suspension.
