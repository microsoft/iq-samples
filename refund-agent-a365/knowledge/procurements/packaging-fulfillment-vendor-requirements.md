## 1. Purpose

This document specifies the packaging, labeling, and fulfillment requirements for all vendors servicing Zava Inc.'s seven production and distribution facilities: Woodinville WA, St. Louis MO, Charlotte NC, Vancouver BC, Montreal QC, Bergamo Italy, and Borås Sweden. All vendors must meet these standards to maintain Approved Vendor status.

## 2. Eco-Friendly Packaging Mandates

Zava is committed to achieving **100% recyclable packaging by December 31, 2026**. All packaging vendors must comply with the following:

- **Primary Packaging (consumer-facing):** Must be composed of ≥ 90% post-consumer recycled (PCR) materials or FSC-certified virgin fiber. No single-use plastics, styrofoam, or PVC.
- **Secondary Packaging (shipping cartons):** Corrugated cardboard, minimum 40% recycled content, single-wall C-flute or double-wall BC-flute depending on product weight.
- **Polybags:** Only LDPE #4 recyclable polybags or compostable PLA film alternatives are permitted. Polybags must include the "How2Recycle" label.
- **Void Fill:** Recycled kraft paper or molded pulp inserts only. No plastic air pillows, bubble wrap, or packing peanuts.
- **Ink and Printing:** Soy-based or water-based inks only. No solvent-based inks on any packaging component.

Vendors must provide annual sustainability certifications and participate in Zava's semi-annual packaging audit.

## 3. Labeling Specifications

Each unit must bear the following labels:

- **Product Label:** SKU, size, color code, lot number, country of origin, fiber content (per FTC Textile Fiber Products Identification Act and EU Regulation 1007/2011).
- **Care Label:** ISO 3758 care symbols, including Zava-specific instructions for sensor garment washing (max 30°C, no tumble dry, no bleach).
- **Smart Product Label:** QR code linking to product registration, warranty activation, and sensor calibration instructions.
- **Barcode:** GS1-128 barcode on outer polybag; UPC-A or EAN-13 on hang tag.
- **RFID:** Passive UHF RFID tag (EPC Gen2, RAIN RFID compliant) embedded in the hang tag. Each tag must be encoded with Zava's SGTIN-96 format and verified readable at ≥ 2 meters.

## 4. Packing Slip and Documentation Standards

- Every carton must include a printed packing slip listing: PO number, SKU, quantity, lot number, destination facility code, and ship date.
- An Advance Ship Notice (ASN) must be transmitted via EDI 856 at least 24 hours prior to shipment arrival.
- International shipments must include commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and any applicable textile import documentation.

## 5. Shipping Container Specifications

| Container Type | Use Case | Specification |
|----------------|----------|---------------|
| Standard Carton | Individual store replenishment | Max 20 kg, 600 × 400 × 300 mm, edge crush ≥ 32 ECT |
| Master Carton | Bulk warehouse receiving | Max 25 kg, 600 × 400 × 400 mm, edge crush ≥ 44 ECT |
| Pallet | Full pallet loads | Standard EUR pallet (1200 × 800 mm) or GMA pallet (48″ × 40″), stretch-wrapped, max stack height 1.6 m |

All cartons must be palletized in a uniform stacking pattern. Mixed-SKU pallets require layer separators and a pallet-level contents summary label.

## 6. Vendor Scorecarding

Fulfillment vendors are scored quarterly on the following criteria:

| Metric | Weight | Target |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Order Accuracy | 25% | ≥ 99.5% |
| On-Time Shipment | 25% | ≥ 98% |
| Packaging Compliance | 20% | ≥ 99% |
| ASN Accuracy | 15% | ≥ 98% |
| Sustainability Compliance | 15% | 100% |

Vendors scoring below 90% composite for two consecutive quarters will be placed on probation. Below 85% triggers re-qualification or removal from the Approved Vendor List.
