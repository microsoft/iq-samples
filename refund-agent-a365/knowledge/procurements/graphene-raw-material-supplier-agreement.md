**Document No:** PROC-2026-001

**Review Date:** January 15, 2027

**Approved By:** Zava Materials Procurement Board

## 1. Parties

This Supplier Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Zava Inc.**, headquartered at 14200 Woodinville-Redmond Road, Woodinville, WA 98072, USA, and **Graphene Solutions Ltd**, registered at Unit 7, Graphene Innovation Centre, Manchester M1 3BB, United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope of Supply

Graphene Solutions Ltd ("Supplier") shall serve as the primary supplier of Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD) graphene for Zava's ZavaCore Smart Mesh product line. This material is critical to the structural and conductive properties of all Zava smart fiber products.

## 3. Material Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Graphene Type | CVD-grown monolayer and few-layer graphene |
| Purity | ≥ 99.7% carbon content |
| Sheet Resistance | ≤ 500 Ω/sq (monolayer) |
| Layer Count | 1–3 layers, per order specification |
| Defect Density | < 0.1 defects/μm² (Raman spectroscopy verified) |
| Form Factor | Powder, dispersion, or film — per purchase order |
| Batch Consistency | Coefficient of variation ≤ 2% across lots |

Each shipment must include a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) with Raman spectra, purity assay, and particle size distribution data.

## 4. Delivery Schedule and Minimum Order Quantities

- **Delivery Frequency:** Monthly, by the 10th calendar day of each month.
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** 500 kg per month across all form factors.
- **Maximum Monthly Capacity Reserved:** 2,000 kg (guaranteed allocation).
- **Delivery Locations:** Zava production facilities in Woodinville WA, St. Louis MO, Charlotte NC, Vancouver BC, Montreal QC, Bergamo Italy, and Borås Sweden. Split shipments per Zava's monthly allocation schedule.
- **Lead Time for Non-Standard Orders:** 30 calendar days from purchase order confirmation.

## 5. Pricing Structure

| Monthly Volume (kg) | Price per kg (USD) |

| 500–750 | $1,240 |

Prices are FOB Manchester, exclusive of customs duties and international freight. Pricing is fixed for the first 12 months; annual adjustments are capped at CPI + 2%, with 90 days' written notice.

## 6. Quality and Certification Requirements

- Supplier shall maintain **ISO 9001:2015** certification throughout the term of this Agreement.
- Supplier shall participate in Zava's quarterly vendor audits, including facility inspections and process reviews.
- Any material failing to meet specifications upon Zava's incoming inspection may be rejected and returned at Supplier's expense within 14 business days of receipt.
- Supplier shall maintain batch traceability records for a minimum of 7 years.

## 7. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemics, government sanctions, natural disasters, or other events beyond reasonable control. The affected party must notify the other within 72 hours and provide a remediation plan within 14 calendar days. If a force majeure event persists beyond 90 days, either party may terminate this Agreement without penalty upon 30 days' written notice.

## 8. Contract Term and Renewal

- **Initial Term:** 3 years from the Effective Date (January 15, 2026 – January 14, 2029).
- **Renewal:** Automatically renews for successive 1-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the end of the then-current term.
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice for material breach that remains uncured after a 30-day cure period.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington, USA. Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) in Seattle, WA.
