## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the collection, storage, processing, and sharing of personal and biometric data generated by ZavaCore Smart Mesh products. Zava is committed to protecting the privacy of all users and ensuring compliance with applicable data protection regulations, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all biometric and personal data collected through ZavaCore Smart Mesh garments, the Zava mobile application, zava.com, and any connected fitness platform integrations. It covers both B2C consumers and individual wearers of B2B products (e.g., professional athletes using team-issued ZavaCore apparel).

## 3. Data Collected

ZavaCore Smart Mesh sensors collect the following categories of biometric data when activated and paired with the Zava app:

| Data Category | Specific Metrics | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiovascular | Heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate trends | Biometric / Sensitive |
| Thermoregulation | Body surface temperature, heat zone mapping | Biometric / Sensitive |
| Movement | Acceleration, velocity, stride cadence, distance, GPS location (if enabled) | Personal / Behavioral |
| Muscle Engagement | Electrical signal intensity by muscle group, fatigue indicators | Biometric / Sensitive |
| Device Metadata | Sensor firmware version, battery status, garment ID, Bluetooth pairing logs | Technical / Non-Sensitive |

## 4. Legal Basis and Consent

### 4.1 B2C Customers
Biometric data collection requires **explicit, informed, opt-in consent** prior to sensor activation. Users must affirmatively agree to the Zava Data Collection Terms during app onboarding. Consent may be withdrawn at any time through the Zava app settings, which will immediately cease data collection from the associated garment.

### 4.2 B2B Wearers
For professional athletes and institutional wearers, data collection is governed by the terms of the B2B Data Processing Agreement (DPA) executed between Zava and the contracting organization. The contracting organization is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from individual wearers in accordance with applicable law. Zava acts as a data processor in B2B relationships.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All biometric data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- Data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified cloud infrastructure located in the United States (primary) and the European Union (for EU/UK customers, in compliance with GDPR data residency requirements).
- Access to biometric data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, enforced through role-based access controls and multi-factor authentication.
- Annual third-party penetration testing and security audits are conducted.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Biometric data:** Retained for **24 months** from the date of collection, after which it is automatically anonymized or deleted.
- **Account and transaction data:** Retained for 7 years per financial record-keeping requirements.
- **Anonymized aggregate data:** May be retained indefinitely for product improvement and research purposes.

## 7. Right to Deletion

Users may request deletion of their biometric and personal data at any time by submitting a request through the Zava app, emailing privacy@zava.com, or calling 1-888-ZAVA-HELP. Deletion requests are processed within **30 calendar days**. Upon deletion, all biometric data, workout history, and sensor pairing records are permanently removed from Zava systems. Anonymized aggregate data that cannot be re-identified is exempt from deletion requests.

## 8. Third-Party Data Sharing

### 8.1 B2C Customers
Zava does **not** sell biometric data. Personal data is shared with third parties only with the user's **explicit consent** (e.g., syncing data to Apple Health, Google Fit, or Strava). Users control integrations through the Zava app and may revoke access at any time.

### 8.2 B2B Partners
Data sharing with B2B partners (teams, coaches, training staff) is governed by the applicable Data Processing Agreement. Zava provides data only to authorized contacts designated by the contracting organization.

### 8.3 Service Providers
Zava engages vetted service providers (cloud hosting, analytics, customer support) under binding data processing agreements that prohibit independent use of personal data.

## 9. User Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable laws, users have the right to: access their data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing. Requests may be submitted to privacy@zava.com and will be fulfilled within 30 days.
