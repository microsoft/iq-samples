## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the warranty coverage, claim process, and liability limitations for all ZavaCore Smart Mesh products sold through authorized channels. Zava stands behind the quality and performance of its products and provides warranty protection against manufacturing defects and premature component failure.

## 2. Warranty Coverage

### 2.1 B2C Products — 1-Year Limited Warranty
All ZavaCore consumer products (Premium and Elite tier athletic tops, shorts, and pants) are covered by a **1-year limited warranty** from the date of original purchase. This warranty covers:

- **Sensor malfunction:** Embedded nano sensors that fail to accurately capture biometric data (heart rate, body temperature, movement, muscle engagement) within specified tolerances under normal use conditions
- **Graphene mesh degradation:** Loss of conductivity, delamination, or structural breakdown of the ZavaCore Smart Mesh fabric that impairs sensor function or garment integrity
- **Seam and construction failure:** Seam splitting, stitching unraveling, or bonding failure under normal use conditions
- **Premature color fading:** Significant color loss that occurs within the warranty period under adherence to published care instructions

### 2.2 B2B Products — 2-Year Extended Warranty
ZavaCore B2B Systems products—including professional sports jerseys and smart cleat systems—are covered by a **2-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery. B2B warranty terms may be extended or modified per the applicable Master Service Agreement (MSA). The 2-year coverage includes all B2C warranty items plus:

- **Firmware and connectivity failures** in smart cleat sensor modules
- **Team logo and custom printing durability** (delamination or cracking under normal athletic use)
- **Bulk replacement guarantee:** If more than 5% of a single B2B order exhibits the same defect, Zava will replace the entire batch at no cost

## 3. What Is Not Covered

This warranty does not cover:

- **Normal wear and tear**, including gradual sensor accuracy reduction after the rated lifespan (100+ wash cycles) or fabric pilling
- **Misuse or abuse**, including use in conditions outside the product's intended purpose (e.g., non-athletic industrial use, exposure to corrosive chemicals)
- **Unauthorized modifications**, including cutting, sewing, or altering the garment in a way that affects sensor placement or mesh continuity
- **Failure to follow care instructions**, including machine washing on hot/heavy cycles, bleaching, or ironing over sensor zones
- **Damage from third-party accessories** or devices not certified for use with ZavaCore products
- **Cosmetic damage** that does not affect product functionality (minor scratches, surface scuffs on smart cleat housings)

## 4. Warranty Claim Process

1. **Initiate a Claim:** Contact Zava at 1-888-ZAVA-HELP, visit support.zava.com, or email warranty@zava.com. Provide your order number, product details, and a description of the issue with photographs.
2. **Evaluation:** Zava's warranty team will review the claim within **5 business days**. Additional diagnostic information (e.g., Zava app sensor logs) may be requested.
3. **Return Authorization:** If the claim is approved, a prepaid return label will be issued. Ship the defective item within 14 days of receiving the return authorization.
4. **Resolution:** Upon inspection, Zava will repair the item, provide a replacement of equal or greater value, or issue a refund at Zava's discretion. Replacements are shipped via Express delivery at no cost. Resolution is typically completed within **10–15 business days** of receiving the returned item.

## 5. Proof of Purchase

A valid proof of purchase (order confirmation email, receipt, or Zava account order history) is required for all warranty claims. Products purchased from unauthorized resellers or secondary markets are not eligible for warranty coverage.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law:

- Zava's total liability under this warranty shall not exceed the **original purchase price** of the product.
- Zava is **not liable** for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including but not limited to loss of data, loss of revenue, personal injury claims arising from reliance on biometric data for medical decisions, or business interruption.
- ZavaCore biometric data is intended for **fitness and athletic performance purposes only** and is not a medical device. Users should not rely on ZavaCore data for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
