## 1. Purpose

This document defines Zava's shipping service levels, delivery timelines, carrier partnerships, and performance standards for all domestic and international shipments. It establishes accountability measures for fulfillment operations and outlines remedies for service failures.

## 2. Shipping Tiers — Domestic (United States & Canada)

| Service Level | Estimated Transit Time | Cost (B2C) | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days | Free on orders $75+ / $6.95 otherwise | All orders |
| Express Shipping | 2–3 business days | $14.95 | All orders |
| Next Day Shipping | 1 business day | $24.95 | Orders placed before 1:00 PM PT, Mon–Fri |
| B2B Bulk Freight | 7–14 business days | Per contract | Orders exceeding 500 units |

All transit times are calculated from the date of shipment, not the date of order. Orders are typically processed and shipped within 1–2 business days of placement.

## 3. International Shipping

| Region | Standard Transit | Express Transit |
|---|---|---|
| Europe (EU/UK) | 7–12 business days | 3–5 business days |
| Canada | 5–9 business days | 2–4 business days |

International customers are responsible for all applicable customs duties, import taxes, and brokerage fees. Zava is not liable for delays caused by customs processing.

## 4. Carrier Partners

Zava partners with the following carriers to ensure reliable fulfillment:
- **UPS** — Primary domestic carrier for Standard and Express shipments
- **FedEx** — Next Day and time-sensitive Express shipments
- **USPS** — Lightweight and rural area deliveries
- **DHL Express** — International shipments to Europe and Canada
- **XPO Logistics** — B2B bulk freight and pallet shipments

Carrier selection is determined automatically based on package weight, destination, service level, and cost optimization. Zava reserves the right to change carriers without prior notice.

## 5. Delivery Confirmation

All shipments include tracking and delivery confirmation. Signature confirmation is required for orders exceeding $250 in value and all B2B shipments. Customers may waive signature requirements through their Zava account settings for residential deliveries.

## 6. B2B Service Level Agreements and Penalties

B2B orders are subject to the following performance commitments:

- **On-Time Delivery Rate:** 97% of B2B orders delivered within the agreed timeline per quarter
- **Order Accuracy Rate:** 99.5% of B2B orders shipped with correct items, quantities, and configurations
- **Late Delivery Credit:** B2B customers are entitled to a **5% credit** on the affected order value for each business day an order is delayed beyond the contracted delivery window, up to a maximum of **25%**
- **Fulfillment Error Resolution:** Replacement shipments for fulfillment errors are dispatched within 2 business days via Express shipping at Zava's expense

SLA performance is reviewed quarterly and reported to each B2B account manager.

## 7. Lost and Damaged Package Procedures

### 7.1 Lost Packages
A package is classified as lost if tracking shows no update for **5 consecutive business days** after the last scan, or if it has not been delivered within **14 business days** of shipment. Customers should report lost packages through the Zava Support Portal or by calling 1-888-ZAVA-HELP. Zava will initiate a carrier investigation and, if the package cannot be located within 5 business days, will reship the order or issue a full refund at the customer's preference.

### 7.2 Damaged Packages
Customers who receive damaged shipments must report the damage within **48 hours** of delivery with photographic evidence. Zava will arrange a replacement shipment or refund at no cost to the customer.

## 8. Shipping Restrictions

Zava does not ship to P.O. boxes for Express or Next Day orders. Certain ZavaCore products containing lithium-ion sensor components may be restricted from air freight to specific international destinations per IATA regulations.

## 9. Severe Weather and Force Majeure

Zava monitors weather conditions across all shipping corridors. In the event of severe weather or force majeure events, estimated delivery dates may be extended. Customers affected by significant weather-related delays (7+ business days beyond the estimated delivery date) may be eligible for a refund under the Zava Refund and Return Policy (POL-CS-2026-001).
