# Zava Inc. — Environmental Sustainability Policy

**Document #:** POL-ESG-2026-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Revised:** December 5, 2025
**Policy Owner:** Sustainability & Corporate Responsibility
**Approved By:** Kayo Miwa, Chief Executive Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy articulates Zava's commitment to environmental sustainability across all aspects of our operations—from raw material sourcing and manufacturing to product delivery and end-of-life management. As a leader in smart materials innovation, Zava recognizes our responsibility to minimize environmental impact while delivering high-performance products to athletes worldwide.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Zava operations globally, including corporate offices (headquartered in Woodinville, WA), manufacturing facilities, distribution centers, retail locations, and the activities of our supply chain partners and contract manufacturers.

## 3. Carbon Neutrality Commitment

Zava is committed to achieving **carbon neutrality across all operations by 2028**. Key initiatives include:

- **Scope 1 & 2 Emissions:** Transition to 100% renewable energy at all owned and operated facilities by Q4 2027. Current progress: 72% renewable as of January 2026.
- **Scope 3 Emissions:** Reduce supply chain and logistics emissions by 40% (from 2023 baseline) by 2030 through carrier optimization, route efficiency programs, and supplier decarbonization partnerships.
- **Carbon Offsets:** Invest in verified carbon offset programs (Gold Standard and Verra VCS certified) for residual emissions that cannot be eliminated through operational changes.
- **Annual Reporting:** Publish an annual Sustainability Report with independently audited emissions data aligned to the GHG Protocol.

## 4. Packaging and Waste Reduction

- **100% recyclable packaging** across all B2C and B2B product shipments by Q2 2026. All polybags are being replaced with compostable mailers made from plant-based materials.
- **Zero single-use plastic** in Zava-branded packaging by end of 2026.
- **Warehouse waste diversion rate** target: 90% by 2027 (landfill diversion through recycling, composting, and material recovery).
- **Shipping material optimization:** Right-sized packaging program reduces void fill and corrugated material usage by an estimated 30%.

## 5. Water Stewardship

Graphene mesh production and textile dyeing are water-intensive processes. Zava is committed to responsible water management:

- **Reduce water consumption** in manufacturing by 25% per unit produced by 2028 (from 2024 baseline).
- **Zero discharge** of untreated wastewater from all Zava-operated and contract manufacturing facilities. All facilities must meet or exceed local wastewater treatment standards.
- **Closed-loop water recycling** systems required at all graphene processing facilities by 2027.

## 6. Responsible Graphene Sourcing

Zava's competitive advantage depends on high-quality graphene raw materials. We are committed to sourcing responsibly:

- All graphene suppliers must comply with Zava's **Responsible Sourcing Code**, which mandates safe handling practices, environmental impact assessments, and fair labor standards.
- Suppliers must provide **chain of custody documentation** for graphene materials, confirming origin, processing methods, and environmental compliance.
- Annual third-party audits of Tier 1 graphene suppliers. Zava reserves the right to terminate contracts with suppliers who fail to meet environmental or ethical standards after a remediation period.

## 7. ZavaReNew — Product End-of-Life Program

Zava's **ZavaReNew** program provides customers with a responsible end-of-life solution for ZavaCore products:

- Customers may return any ZavaCore garment—regardless of condition—to any Zava retail location or through the ZavaReNew prepaid mail-in program.
- Returned garments are sorted for **refurbishment and resale** (Zava Renewed collection), **material recovery** (graphene fiber reclamation and textile recycling), or **responsible disposal**.
- Customers who return a garment through ZavaReNew receive a **$15 Zava store credit** toward their next purchase.
- Goal: Divert **50,000 garments** from landfills annually by 2027.

## 8. Supplier Environmental Standards

All Zava suppliers and contract manufacturers must:

- Maintain an environmental management system aligned to **ISO 14001** or equivalent
- Report annual energy consumption, water usage, waste generation, and greenhouse gas emissions to Zava
- Participate in Zava's Supplier Sustainability Scorecard program, with performance reviewed annually
- Demonstrate continuous improvement toward agreed environmental targets

Suppliers scoring below the minimum threshold on the Sustainability Scorecard for two consecutive review periods will be placed on a corrective action plan. Failure to improve may result in contract termination.

## 9. Employee Engagement

Zava encourages all employees to contribute to our sustainability goals through the **Green Team** volunteer program, office and facility sustainability initiatives, and by incorporating environmental considerations into product design and business decisions.

## 10. Governance and Accountability

The VP of Sustainability reports directly to the CEO and provides quarterly progress updates to the Board of Directors. Sustainability targets are integrated into executive compensation metrics beginning FY2026.
