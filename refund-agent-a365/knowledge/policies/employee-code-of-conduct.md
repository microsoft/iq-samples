# Zava Inc. — Employee Code of Conduct

**Document #:** POL-HR-2026-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Revised:** November 15, 2025
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources & Legal
**Approved By:** Kayo Miwa, Chief Executive Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the behavioral and ethical standards expected of all Zava employees, contractors, and temporary workers. As a company at the forefront of smart materials innovation, Zava's continued success depends on the integrity, professionalism, and accountability of every team member across our 10,110-person global workforce.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Zava employees worldwide, including full-time, part-time, contract, and temporary personnel, across all offices, manufacturing facilities, retail locations, and remote work environments. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 3. Ethical Standards and Integrity

All employees must conduct business with honesty, transparency, and in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Employees shall:

- Never engage in fraud, bribery, corruption, or deceptive practices
- Accurately report financial data, time records, and expense claims
- Comply with all international trade regulations, anti-money laundering laws, and sanctions requirements
- Report suspected violations of law or company policy through the Zava Ethics Hotline (anonymous: 1-800-ZAVA-ETH) or to their manager or HR Business Partner

Zava maintains a strict **non-retaliation policy**. Employees who report concerns in good faith are protected from adverse employment action.

## 4. Anti-Harassment and Inclusive Workplace

Zava is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying. Prohibited conduct includes:

- Harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic
- Sexual harassment, including unwelcome advances, requests for favors, and inappropriate verbal or physical conduct
- Bullying, intimidation, or hostile behavior in any work setting, including virtual environments

All complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially by the HR Investigations team. Substantiated violations result in corrective action up to and including termination.

## 5. Intellectual Property Protection

Zava's proprietary graphene smart fiber technology, ZavaCore Smart Mesh manufacturing processes, sensor algorithms, and product designs represent critical competitive advantages. All employees must:

- Maintain strict confidentiality of trade secrets, proprietary formulas, manufacturing processes, and unreleased product information
- Comply with all Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreements signed at hire
- Never share confidential information with external parties, including friends, family, former employers, or on social media, without explicit written authorization from Zava Legal
- Return all company materials, documents, and data upon separation from employment
- Report any suspected intellectual property theft or unauthorized disclosure immediately to the Legal department

Unauthorized disclosure of Zava's proprietary graphene mesh formulations or sensor technology is grounds for immediate termination and may result in civil or criminal legal action.

## 6. Conflict of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict—or appear to conflict—with Zava's interests. Employees must disclose any financial interest in, or employment or consulting relationship with, any Zava competitor, supplier, or business partner. Outside employment in the smart materials, athletic apparel, or wearable technology industries requires prior written approval from the employee's VP and HR.

## 7. Social Media Policy

Employees may discuss their general work experience on social media but must never disclose confidential business information, unreleased products, proprietary technology, financial results, or internal strategies. All social media posts must include a disclaimer that views expressed are personal and do not represent Zava. Employees designated as Zava brand ambassadors must follow the Social Media Brand Guidelines issued by the Marketing team.

## 8. Sustainability and Corporate Responsibility

Zava is committed to environmental sustainability and responsible business practices. All employees are expected to support Zava's sustainability goals (see POL-ESG-2026-001), minimize waste in their work, and report environmental concerns. Employees involved in product development and manufacturing must prioritize sustainable materials and processes whenever technically and economically feasible.

## 9. Compliance and Reporting

Employees are responsible for reading, understanding, and complying with this Code of Conduct. Annual acknowledgment is required as part of the performance review cycle. Questions or concerns should be directed to HR, Legal, or the Zava Ethics Hotline.
