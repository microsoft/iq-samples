## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the quality assurance standards, testing protocols, and acceptance criteria for all ZavaCore Smart Mesh products. It ensures that every product shipped to customers—whether B2C athletic apparel or B2B professional sports systems—meets Zava's rigorous performance and safety requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all products incorporating ZavaCore Smart Mesh technology, including Premium and Elite tier athletic tops, shorts, and pants, as well as B2B professional sports jerseys and smart cleat systems. All contract manufacturers, fabric suppliers, and sensor component vendors must comply with these standards.

## 3. ZavaCore Smart Mesh Sensor Standards

### 3.1 Sensor Calibration
All embedded nano sensors must be factory-calibrated to the following accuracy tolerances prior to shipment:
- **Heart rate detection:** ±2 BPM accuracy at rest; ±5 BPM during high-intensity activity
- **Body temperature:** ±0.3°F (±0.15°C)
- **Movement tracking (accelerometer):** ±2% deviation across three axes
- **Muscle engagement mapping:** ±5% signal fidelity under load

Sensors must pass a 60-second automated calibration test during final assembly. Units failing calibration are rerouted for rework or rejected.

### 3.2 Graphene Mesh Integrity Testing
Every production lot undergoes graphene mesh conductivity testing to verify continuous electrical pathways across the garment. Acceptable resistance variance is ≤5% from the design specification. Mesh integrity is verified at three stages: raw material receiving, post-weaving, and post-assembly.

## 4. Durability Testing

### 4.1 Wash Durability
ZavaCore products must maintain full sensor functionality through a minimum of **100 machine wash cycles** (cold water, gentle cycle) and **50 tumble dry cycles** (low heat). Sensor accuracy degradation of no more than 10% is acceptable after 100 washes. Products are tested per AATCC Test Method 135.

### 4.2 Color Fastness
All dyed fabrics must achieve a minimum rating of **4 on the Gray Scale** (AATCC Evaluation Procedure 1) for both wash fastness and perspiration fastness. Elite tier products must achieve a rating of 4–5.

### 4.3 Seam Strength
Seam burst strength must meet or exceed **45 lbs/in (7.9 kg/cm)** per ASTM D3787. High-stress seams (shoulder, inseam, underarm) are tested at 1.5× the standard threshold. All sensor-integrated seams must maintain electrical continuity after stress testing.

### 4.4 Size Accuracy
Finished garment measurements must fall within **±0.5 inches (±1.3 cm)** of the graded specification for all critical measurements (chest, waist, inseam, sleeve length).

## 5. Defect Classification

| Classification | Definition | Examples | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Safety hazard or complete sensor failure | Sensor overheating, electrical short, skin irritation from materials | 100% rejection; lot quarantine and root cause investigation |
| **Major** | Significant functional or aesthetic defect | Sensor inaccuracy >15%, visible graphene mesh delamination, incorrect sizing >1 inch | Reject; rework if feasible |
| **Minor** | Cosmetic imperfection with no functional impact | Minor thread irregularity, slight color variation within tolerance, packaging scuff | Accept with documentation; maximum 2.5% AQL per lot |

## 6. Inspection and Sampling

- **Incoming Material Inspection:** AQL 1.0 sampling per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 for all graphene fiber and sensor component shipments
- **In-Process Inspection:** 100% sensor calibration; visual inspection at AQL 2.5
- **Final Audit:** AQL 1.5 for B2B orders; AQL 2.5 for B2C orders
- **Third-Party Testing:** Annual certification by an accredited testing lab (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 compliance)

## 7. Continuous Improvement

All defect data is tracked in Zava's QMS (Quality Management System). Monthly defect trend reviews are conducted by the QA Engineering team, with corrective action plans (CAPAs) initiated for any recurring defect category exceeding established thresholds. Supplier scorecards are reviewed quarterly.
