## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the terms and conditions governing product returns, exchanges, and refunds for all Zava consumer (B2C) products, including ZavaCore Smart Mesh apparel and accessories. Zava is committed to ensuring every customer has a positive experience with our products and will process all eligible returns and refunds promptly and fairly.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all B2C purchases made through zava.com, the Zava mobile app, authorized retail partners, and Zava flagship stores. B2B contract orders (professional sports jerseys, smart cleat systems, and bulk institutional orders) are governed by the terms specified in each customer's Master Service Agreement (MSA) and are excluded from this policy.

## 3. Standard Return Policy

### 3.1 Return Window
Customers may return unworn, unused items in original packaging with all tags attached within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date for a full refund of the purchase price. Items must be in resalable condition with no evidence of wear, washing, or sensor activation beyond initial setup.

### 3.2 Defective Product Exchanges
ZavaCore Smart Mesh products with confirmed sensor defects, graphene mesh degradation, or manufacturing defects are eligible for exchange within **90 calendar days** of the delivery date. Defective items do not need to be in original packaging but must be returned for quality analysis. Zava will provide a prepaid return shipping label for all defective product exchanges.

### 3.3 How to Initiate a Return or Exchange
Customers may initiate a return or exchange through any of the following channels:
- **Zava Support Portal:** returns.zava.com
- **Customer Service Phone:** 1-888-ZAVA-HELP (1-888-928-2435), available Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–7:00 PM PT
- **Email:** returns@zava.com
- **In-Store:** At any Zava flagship location with proof of purchase

A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number will be issued within one business day. Items returned without an RMA number may experience processing delays.

## 4. Severe Weather Delay Refunds

Zava **DOES** authorize full refunds for shipments that experience severe delays (defined as **7+ business days beyond the estimated delivery date**) caused by weather events including but not limited to hurricanes, blizzards, flooding, wildfires, and extreme temperature events. Customers do not need to return the product to receive a weather-delay refund. Weather delay refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** of approval.

### 4.1 Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a weather-delay refund, the following conditions must be met:
- The shipment must be delayed by **7 or more business days** beyond the original estimated delivery date shown at the time of order.
- The delay must be attributable to a documented severe weather event affecting the shipping route or destination, as verified by carrier tracking data and/or National Weather Service (NWS) alerts.
- The customer must submit a refund request within **30 calendar days** of the original estimated delivery date.

### 4.2 Refund Amount for Weather Delays
Weather-delay refunds include the **full purchase price plus original shipping costs**. If the customer elects to keep the product upon eventual delivery, no return is required. If the customer has already received a replacement order, Zava may request the return of the delayed shipment at Zava's expense.

### 4.3 Verification Process
Zava's logistics team will verify the weather delay against carrier records and public weather advisories. Approval or denial will be communicated within **3 business days** of the request submission.

## 5. Refund Amounts and Methods

| Return Type | Refund Amount | Shipping Costs Refunded | Restocking Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard return (within 14 days) | Full purchase price | No | None |
| Standard return (15–30 days) | Full purchase price | No | 15% |
| Defective product exchange | Full purchase price or replacement | Yes | None |
| Severe weather delay | Full purchase price | Yes | None |

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Credit card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days. Gift card purchases are refunded as Zava store credit.

## 6. Restocking Fees

- **No restocking fee** applies to returns made within 14 calendar days of delivery, defective product returns, or weather-delay refunds.
- A **15% restocking fee** applies to standard returns initiated between 15 and 30 calendar days after delivery.
- Restocking fees are deducted from the refund amount prior to processing.

## 7. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or refund under this policy:
- Custom B2B orders, including team jerseys with custom printing, logos, or personalization
- Items marked as **"Final Sale"** or purchased from the Zava Clearance outlet
- Gift cards and promotional credits
- ZavaCore sensor replacement kits that have been opened or activated
- Items damaged due to misuse, unauthorized modification, or failure to follow care instructions

## 8. Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color are available within the 30-day return window at no additional cost, subject to availability. Exchange orders are shipped via Standard shipping at no charge. Customers requiring expedited exchange shipping may select a faster method at their expense.

## 9. International Returns

Customers outside the United States are responsible for return shipping costs and any applicable customs duties or taxes unless the return is due to a product defect or Zava fulfillment error. International weather-delay refunds follow the same eligibility criteria as domestic orders.

## 10. B2B Orders

Returns and refunds for B2B contract customers—including professional sports teams, athletic programs, and institutional buyers—are governed exclusively by the terms outlined in the applicable Master Service Agreement (MSA). B2B customers should contact their designated Zava Account Manager or email b2b-support@zava.com for return or refund inquiries.

## 11. Policy Amendments

Zava reserves the right to amend this policy at any time. Changes will be published on zava.com and take effect 30 days after posting. Orders placed prior to a policy change are governed by the policy in effect at the time of purchase.
